Log in to your Peacock TV account to stream movies, TV shows, live sports, news, and exclusive Peacock Originals on your favorite devices.

===========================

Follow these simple steps to access your Peacock account:

1. Open your web browser and visit the official Peacock website.
2. Click the **Sign In** button in the upper-right corner.
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Type your account password.
5. Click **Sign In** to access your Peacock account. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

Sign In on Smart TV
===================

To sign in on a Smart TV or streaming device:

1. Open the Peacock app.
2. Select **Sign In**.
3. An activation code will appear on your TV.
4. On your phone or computer, visit the official Peacock device activation page.
5. Sign in to your account and enter the activation code.
6. Your TV will automatically refresh and log you in. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Common Login Problems
=====================

You may experience login issues if:

- Incorrect email or password is entered.
- Caps Lock is enabled while typing the password.
- Your password has been changed recently.
- Your internet connection is unstable.
- Your browser cache or app data needs to be cleared. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Reset Your Password
===================

If you forgot your password:

1. Select **Forgot Password?** on the login page.
2. Enter your registered email address.
3. Check your inbox for the password reset email.
4. Follow the instructions to create a new password.
5. Sign in using your updated credentials. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Tips for a Secure Login
=======================

- Use a strong, unique password.
- Never share your login credentials.
- Keep your browser and Peacock app updated.
- Log out from public or shared devices.
- Enable secure internet connections whenever possible.

Conclusion
==========

Logging into Peacock TV is quick and simple on both web browsers and supported devices. If you experience sign-in problems, verify your login credentials, reset your password if needed, or use the device activation process for Smart TVs. Following these steps will help you access your Peacock account securely and start streaming without interruption.
